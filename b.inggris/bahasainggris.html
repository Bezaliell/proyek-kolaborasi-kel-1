How Used Keyboards Are Transformed into Automatic Night Lights

Electronic waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the rapid development of technology. Old computer keyboards are often discarded when they are damaged or no longer needed. However, these keyboards still contain materials that can be reused. One creative way to manage this waste is by transforming used keyboards into automatic night lights. This product combines environmental awareness, creativity, and simple electronic technology.

The automatic night light consists of several main components. The base uses materials from an old keyboard as a support and decoration. The lamp body is made of transparent material to allow light to spread properly. The electronic circuit uses several components, including an LED as the light source, an LDR sensor to detect light intensity, a resistor to limit electric current, a transistor as an electronic switch, and a battery as the power source.

The working process begins when the LDR sensor detects the light conditions around it. Changes in light intensity affect the signal in the transistor circuit. When the surrounding environment becomes dark, the transistor activates the electric current flowing to the LED, causing the light to turn on automatically. On the other hand, when the environment becomes bright, the LED turns off according to the circuit settings. This process allows the lamp to operate automatically without using a microcontroller.

As a result, used keyboards can be transformed into automatic night lights that have both functional and aesthetic value. This product can be used as additional lighting and room decoration. It also provides an opportunity to learn about simple electronic circuits, especially the use of LDR sensors and transistors. Furthermore, this innovation helps reduce electronic waste and encourages creativity and environmental awareness. The production of this automatic night light applies the 3R principles (Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle) to support a cleaner and more sustainable environment.

How to Make an Automatic Lamp from an Old Keyboard

Electronic waste, like broken computer keyboards, is a growing problem in modern society. However, these keyboards still contain useful parts that can be repurposed. One creative way to manage this waste is by turning an old keyboard into a unique automatic decorative lamp. This project helps reduce electronic trash while creating something new.

First, an unused keyboard is carefully disassembled by hand. The plastic keys (like W, A, S, D) and frame are separated from the electronic circuits and cleaned from dust. Next, an automatic sensor and an LED light are installed inside a transparent box. This makes the lamp turn on and off automatically.

The cleaned keys are attached to the outside of the lamp frame using strong glue. The glowing LED lights and keyboard buttons create a modern, technological look. Once assembled and dry, it becomes a complete automatic lamp ready to use. Every key is arranged neatly to maximize its aesthetic appeal.

As a result, the old keyboard turns into a functional and aesthetic lamp. It serves as a creative room decoration while saving energy with its sensor. Recycling computer components is a great way to support a sustainable environment. Everyone can try this easy project at home.

How an Old Keyboard Is Transformed into an Automatic Lamp

Electronic waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the rapid development of technology. Old computer keyboards are often discarded when they are damaged or no longer needed. However, some parts of these keyboards can still be reused. One creative way to recycle this waste is by transforming old keyboard keys into an automatic lamp. This product combines creativity, technology, and environmental awareness.

The transformation begins by preparing unused keyboard keys and other materials needed to make the lamp. The keyboard keys are separated from the keyboard and cleaned to remove dust and dirt. After that, the keys are arranged as decorations on the transparent lamp body. Other components are also prepared to support the lighting system and automatic function of the lamp.

The cleaned keyboard keys are attached to the transparent lamp body to create an attractive design. The lamp body is then connected to the supporting structure and the black base. Lighting components are placed inside the transparent part so that the light can spread throughout the lamp. After all components are assembled properly, they form a unique automatic lamp with a modern appearance.

As a result, unused keyboard keys are transformed into an automatic lamp that has functional and aesthetic value. The lamp can be used as a room decoration and provides lighting when needed through its automatic system. This product also helps reduce electronic waste by reusing old keyboard components. Therefore, turning unused keyboard parts into an automatic lamp is a creative way to recycle electronic waste and support environmental sustainability.
